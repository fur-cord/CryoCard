Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

1What we collect

  • Your Discord user ID and username
  • Your Minecraft username and UUID
  • Kill and death counts and the stats shown on your card
  • Your Mixi Coins balance, purchased cosmetics, and the time you last used /daily

We don't collect emails, passwords, payment details, or your IP address. The bot does not record voice chat; it only plays a sound in the channel.

2Why we collect it

Only to run the features: linking your Minecraft account to Discord, building your stats card, tracking your balance, and deciding minigame winners.

3Sharing

We never sell or share your data with third parties. Your stats card is visible to other people in the Discord servers where you or others use the bot's commands.

4Storage

Data is stored on a server run by the developer and kept only as long as it's needed for the features above.

5Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data at any time by contacting us on Discord. Removing the bot from a server or uninstalling the mod stops any new data being collected.

6Children

CryoCard follows Discord's minimum age requirements. We don't knowingly collect data from people who are too young to use Discord.

7Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions can go to RAIDEN on Discord.
